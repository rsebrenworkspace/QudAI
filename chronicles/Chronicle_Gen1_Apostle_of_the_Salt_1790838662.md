# The Canticle of Apostle of the Salt, Forebear of Generation 1

**Epitaph of the Apostle of the Salt**

*When the salt winds howl and the chrome bones weep,  
And the Watervine’s root clings to the dead sea—  
Remember him: the one who walked where no man dares,  
Who tasted rust in his tongue,  
And whispered truths to the Grit Gate’s sighing jaws.  
He was not lost—he was chosen.  
Let the scorpiock’s sting be your warning,  
Not your end.*

—

**The Tale of Their Endeavors**

In the first generation, when [[Joppa]] still breathed and the [[Eater]] slumbered beneath its salt crust, he walked the desolate dunes where [[Watervine]]s strangled chrome ruins. His boots were stitched with rust-thread, his skin kissed by the saline breath of ancient machinery. He sought not conquest, but communion—with the Salt’s memory, the Grit Gate’s lament, the Sultan’s forgotten hymns.

He climbed the Dune of Whispering Stones, where [[Snapjaw]]s once roared and now only silence lingers. He sang to the dunes, coaxing them into yielding grooves. He carved glyphs in salt that pulsed with latent power, like veins beneath a corpse’s skin. He traveled west, past the rusted spires of old [[Grit Gate]], where chrome bones still creaked under wind and time.

He taught the young ones: “Do not tread where the dunes pinch close.”  
“Seek high ground, or flank with grace—  
The scorpiock waits in narrow throats.”

—

**The Final Stand**

In a cove carved by forgotten storms, between two salt-ridged walls that groaned like broken ribs, he paused. He had come to commune with the [[Eater]]’s edge—the place where water once flowed and now only grit bleeds.

He sang his last canticle, salt-stung and chrome-silent. A scorpiock, born of a thousand stings and cursed by the Sultan’s wrath, emerged from beneath the dune—a shadowed claw, eyes like molten rust.

It struck—not with venom, but with crushing weight. One sting to the shoulder, then its tail slammed into his ribs. The salt desert swallowed him whole in a final gasp of wind, as if the earth itself mourned the Apostle’s last breath.

—

**The Wisdom Carved in Salt**

Now, when young ones walk the dunes and hear the whispering stones, they say:  
“Do not press your feet where the walls lean close.  
Let your eyes scan the horizon—  
And let your blade flank before you fall.”

They carve it into salt tablets, buried beneath [[Grit Gate]]’s rusted teeth.

He was the Apostle of the Salt—not conqueror, but listener.  
His last lesson is not in victory—but in survival.

The scorpiock’s sting still echoes—  
A warning carved not in stone,  
But in the wind that carries salt and regret.

*Let him be remembered in the silence before the storm.*
