# The Canticle of Yromoun, Forebear of Generation 7

**Epitaph of Yromoun, Dust-Scarred Sovereign of the Seventh Generation**

*“He walked where chrome bleeds and salt remembers—  
His boots knew Joppa’s sighs, his blade sang with Watervine’s ghost.  
Four hundred turns he turned the void, yet fell not to axe or Eater,  
But to a shadowed snapjaw’s grin, in the canyon’s throat,  
Where Grit Gate weeps and no Sultan dares tread alone.”*

---

**The Tale of Yromoun’s Endeavors**

Yromoun was born beneath the last light of [[Grit Gate]], his hands stained with rust and salt from childhood. He learned to carve chrome into tools, to read the whispers in Watervine’s dying vines, and to walk the desert’s razor teeth without fear. His journey began near the ruins of Joppa, where he found a broken [[Eater]]-sentry—its jaw still clamped on a forgotten gear—and turned it into his first sigil: *“The Machine That Knows Its Own End.”*

He wandered the salt plains, mapping paths through [[Snapjaw]]-ridden canyons, carving glyphs in obsidian to ward off the dark. He traded chrome for stories from the old ones—how Sultan Zeran once danced with a [[Watervine]] that wept crystal tears, how the Grit Gate still held its breath beneath the dunes.

He climbed the broken spires of Joppa’s final bastion, where wind and rust whispered secrets older than time. He faced no army, only the silence of desolation—and he sang to it in tongues no living soul remembers.

---

**The Final Stand**

In his last turn, Yromoun stood at the lip of a canyon that had swallowed too many souls. His compass flickered—westward, toward the whispering sands where [[Snapjaw]]s slinked like rusted ghosts. He knew the lesson: *“Avoid SW near unknown terrain.”* But he was tired. The salt in his lungs stung like forgotten prayers.

He stepped into the shadow of a crumbling ruin—not to conquer, but to rest. A snapjaw rose from behind a broken gear, its jaw wide enough to swallow stars. Yromoun did not scream. He raised his blade—chrome-woven with Watervine’s last pulse—and met it where the wind forgot how to blow.

He was bitten. Not by greed or malice, but by silence. By the canyon’s hunger. By the rust that remembers all things, and forgets none.

---

**The Wisdom Carved in Salt**

They say Yromoun left no crown, only a blade buried under [[Grit Gate]]’s weeping stones. They say his final lesson is etched not on stone—but in every traveler’s breath: *“Let your feet know the wind before you turn westward.”*

The next generation walks with salt-stung eyes and chrome-dusted hands.  
They sing of Yromoun—not as a victor, but as one who walked alone into the silence  
… and found it hungry for his name.

And so he died—  
Not in battle, not in flame,  
But by a shadow that had no face…  
and the canyon that remembers everything.
