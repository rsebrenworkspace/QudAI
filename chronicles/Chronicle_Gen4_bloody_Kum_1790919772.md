# The Canticle of bloody Kum, Forebear of Generation 4

**Canticle of Kum, Bloody One, Who Walked with Rust and Salt**

*“He who bore the weight of the Sultan’s blade,  
Yet bowed to no salt tide—  
Fell where the [[Snapjaw]] gnashed at dusk,  
And the [[Watervine]] wept silver tears.  
His name was Kum; his blood was chrome,  
His soul a wound in the Grit Gate’s throat.”*

—

**The Tale of Kum’s Endeavors**

Born beneath the rusted ribs of Joppa, where salt choked the air and chrome pulsed like dying veins, Kum rose not with song but with steel. He walked the marshes like a ghost forged from iron, his boots whispering over [[Watervine]] roots that hummed with forgotten hymns. He climbed the broken spires of old machinery, hunting for relics lost to time—rusted gears he’d pry free, glowing cores he’d drag beneath his belt. The Sultan’s decree echoed in his bones: *“Carry salt, carry steel, carry the weight of what remains.”*  
He fought the [[Eater]] in brine-fogged tunnels, carved paths through the [[Grit Gate]]’s teeth, and traded blades for whispers from the old ones who still spoke to rust. He never asked why he walked—only that he walk farther, deeper into the salt’s embrace.

—

**The Final Stand**

When the last tide pulled him toward the marsh’s edge, Kum knew it was the same wound that had claimed so many before: the Snapjaw’s toothed grin beneath the dusk. He’d heard the warning—*Retreat or engage only with ranged support.* But the salt fog thickened; his boots sank into mud, and the [[Watervine]] twisted around him like a lasso of memory.  
He raised his blade—not to strike, but to shield. A Snapjaw lunged, jaws wide as broken gearboxes, teeth that glinted with chrome grit. Kum’s final cry was not for mercy but for salt—*“Let the tide take me!”*—and the beast’s fangs found him before he could turn.

—

**The Wisdom Carved in Salt**

To those who follow:  
Do not linger where the [[Snapjaw]] sings, nor trust the marsh’s whispering vines.  
Salt is your ally; rust, your armor.  
If you must fight, let your ranged hands be swift—  
And never forget:  
*The Grit Gate does not forgive delays.*  

Kum died with his blade still hot,  
His salt-stained soul singing to the last tide.  
He was a man of chrome and grit,  
A bloody ghost who walked too far—and paid the price in blood and brine.

—
